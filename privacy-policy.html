Plunk Pals – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
App: Plunk Pals (Android package com.parbatstudio.plunkpals)
Developer: Pankaj Parbat (Parbat Studio)
Contact: pankajparbat6688@gmail.com

In short. Plunk Pals is a puzzle game. You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is stored on your device. If you agree, the game sends anonymous play statistics to help us improve it. The game shows ads, and the advertising service uses your device's advertising ID to do that. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

Plunk Pals is published by Pankaj Parbat (Parbat Studio), India ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your data, write to pankajparbat6688@gmail.com.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, postal address or account login.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise (GPS) location.
  • No information from other apps on your device.

Permissions the game uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, send play statistics and complete purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising service as described above.
  • Google Play Billing: to sell optional in-game items.

3. How we use the information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To understand how the game is played and to improve levels, difficulty and rewards.
  • To show advertising, which pays for the free game, and to offer optional rewards for watching a video.
  • To deliver and restore purchases.
  • To find and fix errors and to prevent cheating and fraud.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it to make decisions about you, and we do not combine it with information from other sources to identify you.

5. Your choices

  • The privacy question. The first time you play, the game asks whether you accept play statistics and personalized ads. Until you answer, no play statistics are sent and ads are requested as non-personalized.
  • Settings > Privacy. You can change both answers at any time: "Share anonymous stats" and "Personalized ads".
  • Android settings. In Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • No Ads. An optional purchase removes banner and between-level ads. Reward videos that you choose to watch remain available.
  • Delete your game data. Use Settings > Reset Game Data, or uninstall the game.

6. How long data is kept, and how to have it deleted

Game progress stays on your device until you reset or uninstall the game. Play statistics and advertising data are kept by Unity Technologies for the periods described in its privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of data connected with your installation of the game, or to ask what data is held, write to pankajparbat6688@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days and pass your request to our service providers where needed. Because the game has no accounts, we may ask you for information from the game that helps us find your installation.

7. Children

Plunk Pals contains no adult content, and its content is suitable for players of all ages. However, because the game shows advertising and can collect the data described above, it is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age required by the law of your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will have it deleted.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Where we rely on your consent (play statistics and personalized ads), you can withdraw it at any time in the game's Settings. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address above.

9. Security

Data sent from the game is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our service providers take reasonable steps to protect the information.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the game or in its store listing.
